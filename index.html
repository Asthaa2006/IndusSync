Business licensing is fragmented by design

A food stall, a pharmacy counter, a small manufacturing unit — each one runs into the same wall of disconnected systems.

Fragmented ownership

Fire NOC, FSSAI license, pollution clearance and municipal approval each live on separate portals that share no common identity.

No visibility

Applicants can't see which department is sitting on their file, or why — they just wait.

Repeated paperwork

Opening a second outlet means re-submitting identity and training proof that were already verified once.

How a request actually moves

Seven steps, from a two-minute intake to a fully cleared business.

01

Structured intake

Industry, business scale and physical attributes — no free text, nothing for the engine to guess at.

02

Requirement resolution

The rule engine matches your input against a configurable table and returns the exact approvals needed, per department.

03

Profile reuse check

Anything already verified on your profile is marked complete. You're only asked for what's genuinely new.

04

Document collection

You upload against the resolved checklist. The platform checks completeness — not authenticity.

05

Multi-department routing

Each requirement becomes an independent task on the relevant department's queue, running in parallel.

06

Human verification

Officials review and approve or reject with a reason, inside their own dashboard. The platform never decides.

07

One unified status

Your business is cleared once every routed task is approved — tracked in one place, not seven.

What this is — and what it deliberately isn't

A smaller, honest claim beats a big one we can't back.

SetuSaathi does

  • Resolve the exact license set for your specific business
  • Reuse verified, person-bound documents across outlets
  • Route each requirement to the right department, in parallel
  • Give you one place to track every approval

SetuSaathi does not

  • Verify document authenticity — that stays with the department
  • Approve or reject anything — a human official always decides
  • Replace NSWS or state industrial portals for large investments
  • Use DigiLocker or pull any live government database in this build
